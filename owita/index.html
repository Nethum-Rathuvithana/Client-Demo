THE VENUE & STORY

Passion in Every Plate

Located directly at Nagoda Junction along the Kalutara South corridor, Ovita Family Restaurant features an iconic two-story architectural design.

✦ Upper Open-Air Rooftop: Enjoy breezy evening dining under glowing Edison string bulb lights with family and friends.
✦ Ground Floor Kitchen & Counter: High-speed wok station and rapid counter pickup area with dedicated parking for motorbikes and three-wheelers.
✦ Direct Hot Delivery: Fast takeaway packaging engineered to keep food steaming hot until you reach home.
