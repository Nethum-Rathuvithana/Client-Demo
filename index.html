Terms of Admission & Door Policy

1. Age Requirement: Strictly 21 years and above. Physical National Identity Card (NIC) or Passport must be presented upon entry.

2. Dress Code: Smart Sophisticated. No athletic singlets, boardshorts, or open rubber slippers are permitted.

3. Security & Screening: All patrons are subject to security metal detector screening. Weapons, illegal narcotics, or outside beverages are strictly prohibited.

4. Table Allocations: VIP tables are held up to 11:30 PM on event nights unless prior notice is provided to your bottle concierge.

5. Management Discretion: Venue management reserves the absolute right to refuse admission or request departure to preserve guest safety and venue ambiance.

Privacy Policy & Guest Discretion

1. Data Collected: When making table reservations, we collect your name, contact phone/WhatsApp number, and reservation preferences solely to facilitate your venue experience.

2. Confidentiality: Guest contact information is strictly confidential and never sold or shared with external third-party advertisers.

3. Photography & Privacy: Cleopatra employs professional event photographers. If you prefer not to appear in published event galleries, notify our concierge on arrival.
