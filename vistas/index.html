Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if it rains during my outdoor sunset booking?

Vistas features both an expansive open-air timber terrace and a weatherproof covered canopy lounge with panoramic floor-to-ceiling glass. If sudden tropical showers occur, perimeter guests are automatically transitioned to our sheltered nature booths without losing their view or deposit credit.

Is the table minimum spend deductible from food and drinks?

Yes, 100%. The minimum spend is NOT an entry fee. Every rupee of your table minimum spend is credited toward your orders of signature cocktails, wine, champagne, tapas, or dinner platters.

Do you accept walk-ins without reservations?

Yes! Walk-in guests are welcome on a first-come, first-served basis at our central marble bar and cocktail standing tables (zero minimum spend on weekdays). However, front-row perimeter oceanfront couches routinely book out 24 to 48 hours in advance for sunset.

Are there non-alcoholic options on Poya and religious holidays?

On statutory full-moon Poya holidays when alcohol sales are prohibited in Sri Lanka, Vistas hosts our acclaimed "Full Moon Poya Skyline Soirée" featuring award-winning artisanal zero-proof botanicals, fresh king coconut coolers, and oceanfront grilled seafood platters.
